
//Multiple Ending Diffindo Challenge

[MainThread]

Capture Harry
Capture Sprout
Capture challengescoremanager
Capture basecam

Cue ThreadPicker *

WaitFor ThreadPicker

//Release Harry

//Release BaseCam

If ChallengeJustWonFirstTime Cue WonFirstTimeThread
If ChallengeWorseThanBefore Cue WorseThread

[WonFirstTimeThread]

WaitFor WonFirstTimeThread

recapture Harry
recapture Sprout
recapture challengescoremanager
recapture basecam

basecam target flyto Harry *
basecam flyto EndCam01 *

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_14 *SproutDone1
//Well done, Mr. Potter, you have completed my Diffindo Challenge!  The remaining time now becomes your personal high score.

BaseCam Target AttachedTo=harry z=5
BaseCam Locked YawStep=-30 Distance=100 *

Waitfor SproutDone1

challengescoremanager tallychallenge *TallyEnd

harry animate celebrate

Waitfor TallyEnd

trigger levelexit

Release challengescoremanager
Release Sprout
Release basecam
Release Harry
