[FirstTimeThread]

WaitFor FirstTimeThread

recapture Harry
recapture Sprout
recapture challengescoremanager
recapture basecam

Harry runto Point1 *HarryDone1

basecam target flyto Harry staylocked time=1 *
basecam flyto EntryCam01 time=2

Waitfor HarryDone1

Trigger EntryBars *

Harry runto Point2 *

basecam flyto IntroCam01 time=2

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_01 *
//Welcome, Harry Potter, to my Diffindo Challenge. To complete the challenge, you must find the Completion Star, which can be found in a halway just above you.

basecam flyto IntroCam03 time=3

basecam target flytounlocked

basecam target flyto EndStar time=2 *

sleep 1

basecam flyto IntroCam05 time=3

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_02
//Try to get to it as quickly as you can. If the Challenge Timer reaches zero before you have the Completion Star, you lose the challenge

basecam flyto StarCam01 *
basecam target flyto StarTarget01

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_03 *SproutDone1
//Along the way you will find Challenge Stars. Collecting Challenge Stars will boost your timer and score. I highly recommend you try to find all of them.

basecam target flyto Star time=2 *
basecam flyto StarCam02 time=2

basecam flyto StarCam03 time=2

Waitfor SproutDone1

basecam target flyto Harry *
basecam flyto IntroCam04

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_04 *SproutDone2
//Try to get the Completion Star with as many seconds remaining as possible! The higher your score is when you complete the Challenge, the more house points I will award you!

basecam flyto IntroCam02 time=3

Waitfor SproutDone2

basecam target flyto Vines1 time=2 *

sleep 1

basecam flyto Diffindo1 time=2 *

Sprout Talk PC_Spr_Chal3Diff_05 *SproutDone3
//Some vines and plant growth can cover doorways and openings.  You can use Diffindo to cut through them and clear your path.

Waitfor SproutDone3

challengescoremanager beginchallenge *

Release challengescoremanager
Release Sprout
Release Harry
Release basecam

SaveGame
