
//Multiple Ending Skurge Challenge

[MainThread]

Capture Harry
Capture Flitwick
Capture challengescoremanager
Capture basecam

Cue ThreadPicker *

WaitFor ThreadPicker

//Release Harry
//Release Flitwick
//Release challengescoremanager
//Release BaseCam

If ChallengeJustWonFirstTime Cue WonFirstTimeThread
If ChallengeWorseThanBefore Cue WorseThread
If ChallengeNewBestScore Cue NewBestThread

//----------------------------------------------------------------------
[WonFirstTimeThread]

WaitFor WonFirstTimeThread

recapture Harry
recapture Flitwick
recapture challengescoremanager
recapture basecam

Harry walkTo CutMark2 *HarryDone

Flitwick say PC_Flt_Chal2Skurge_41 *
//Well done, Mr Potter, you have completed my Skurge Challenge!  The remaining time now becomes your personal high score.

BaseCam Target AttachedTo=harry z=5
BaseCam Locked YawStep=-30 Distance=100 *

waitfor HarryDone
sleep 2
challengescoremanager tallychallenge *TallyDone
harry animate celebrate

waitfor TallyDone
trigger levelexit
sleep 1

release Flitwick
release basecam
release challengescoremanager
